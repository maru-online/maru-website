## The decision

**The bet:** Maru's next ten clients are SMEs inside corporate supplier-development
programmes, where the **corporate pays** via ESD/enterprise-development budgets —
starting with GrowthIQ's portfolio.

**Why:** the constraint is not the website (≈190 real human visitors in two years — a
denominator problem, not a conversion problem). The constraint is a repeatable source of
clients who can actually pay. INV-000001 (R55,000, cancelled — client could not pay)
proved SMEs want the work but can't fund it. Corporate ESD/B-BBEE budgets are
contractually committed. GrowthIQ is the one live, paying, verified channel into them.

**Rejected (for now):**
- **Option B** (founder-led direct sales into Gauteng professional services) — fallback if
  A's kill criteria fire. Its deposit rule is adopted immediately (see Standing Rules).
- **Option C** (WhatsApp SaaS product) — parked as salvage inventory. No build hours until
  Option A either succeeds (programme SMEs become the pilot cohort, corporate-funded) or
  is killed.
