## Internal positioning statement (not customer copy)

For owner-led South African businesses that handle sensitive client information, Maru Online is the
POPIA-safe AI partner. We put AI to work in the business without sending client data where the law says
it can't go. Unlike generic automation agencies and DIY AI tools, which move data offshore and leave no
audit trail, every Maru build starts with a POPIA-Safe AI Audit and ends with a system the owner can
defend to a client or to the Regulator.

## Who we speak to (ICP)

Owner-led SA businesses, roughly 5–50 staff, whose revenue depends on client personal information. The owner
signs off spend and is, by default, the Information Officer. They already use AI informally, and data runs
through WhatsApp, email and spreadsheets.

- **Channel (H2 2026 Strategy, Option A):** SMEs inside corporate supplier-development (ESD) programmes,
  where the corporate pays. POPIA-safety strengthens this pitch because corporates need suppliers who
  handle data lawfully.
- **Beachhead verticals (direct / Option B fallback):** independent financial advisers and FSPs, private
  medical, dental and allied-health practices, estate and managing agents, small law and accounting firms.
  Choosing the first vertical is still OPEN.

## The legal hooks we may reference (plain language only)

- **POPIA s72:** limits sending personal information outside South Africa. Most AI and automation
  tools store data offshore by default.
- **POPIA s71:** limits decisions based solely on automated processing, such as AI lead scoring or screening.
- **POPIA s109:** administrative fines up to R10 million.
- Enforcement context (cite with source): Information Regulator logged 1,220+ security-compromise
  notifications in five months of 2026 and has proposed immediate administrative fines
  ([Polity, 1 Sep 2026](https://www.polity.org.za/article/the-regulator-is-watching-new-enforcement-signals-for-popia-and-paia-compliance-2026-09-01)).

## Banned claims (legal and credibility risk)

- "Certified POPIA compliant", "POPIA certified", "approved/endorsed by the Information Regulator".
  The Regulator does not certify vendors.
- "Guaranteed compliance", "100% compliant", "fully compliant", "all systems we build are POPIA-compliant".
- Legal advice. Maru is not a law firm. Say "we design for POPIA requirements" and refer legal opinions.
- Unsourced statistics (e.g. "83% of SA businesses are non-compliant").
- Advertising web design, websites, or digital marketing as services. These were sacrificed on 26 Sep 2026.
