## Our guide: AI and POPIA

**What we collect.** When you ask for our guide, we collect your first name and email address. You can also give
your company name. We record whether you ticked the box to receive our notes, the wording you saw, and the time.
We keep a scrambled version of your IP address to stop the form being abused. We do not ask for a phone number.

**Why.** We use your details to send you the guide. If you ticked the box and then confirmed by email, we also send
you practical notes on AI and POPIA. Asking for the guide does not sign you up for anything.

**Who receives it.** We store your request in our own database, hosted by Neon in London, United Kingdom. We use
Brevo to send the guide and, if you asked for them, our notes. Brevo is our operator: it processes your details
for us, under a written agreement, and not for its own purposes. Brevo may process data in [the European Union,]
the United States and India. [Name the safeguard relied on under section 72: for example, Brevo's data processing
agreement and the standard contractual protections in it.]

**How long we keep it.** If you asked for the guide and did not sign up for our notes, we delete your details after
12 months, unless you have since become a client or used our free Exposure Check. If you signed up for our notes,
we keep your details until you unsubscribe.

**Your choices.** Every notes email has a link to unsubscribe, and you can also email hello@maruonline.com. You can
ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, at any time.

**Contact.** Our Information Officer is [full name, as registered with the Information Regulator], reachable at
hello@maruonline.com.
